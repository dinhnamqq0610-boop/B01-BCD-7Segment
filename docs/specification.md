# B01 - BCD to 7-Segment Decoder

## 1. Muc dich
Thiet ke bo giai ma BCD sang LED 7 doan bang Verilog RTL.

## 2. Dau vao
- bcd[3:0]: Ma BCD 4 bit.

## 3. Dau ra
- seg[6:0]: Tin hieu dieu khien 7 doan LED.

## 4. Chuc nang
- Nhan ma BCD tu 0 den 9.
- Giai ma ma BCD thanh tin hieu dieu khien cac doan LED.
- Kiem tra cac ma dau vao khong hop le tu 10 den 15.

## 5. Pham vi
- Thiet ke logic to hop.
- Kiem thu bang testbench.
- Phan tich waveform va thuc hien flow RTL-to-GDSII.
- Khong bao gom che tao chip hoac tape-out.
